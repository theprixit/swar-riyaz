Record and upload your 12 keys
  1. Open your phone's voice recorder. Hold the phone 30 to 40 cm from the grille, main stop pulled, drones pushed in.
  2. Steady bellows. Play Sa, hold about 2 seconds, lift, wait 1 second of silence.
  3. Then each next key going up, one at a time, same way: komal Re, Re, komal Ga, Ga, Ma, tivra Ma, Pa, komal Dha, Dha, komal Ni, Ni. Twelve notes, in order. Upper Sa at the end is optional.
  4. Stop recording and pick the file below. The page splits it, checks the pitch of every key against your Sa, and tells you if a key is off or missing.

Voice Memos (.m4a), .mp3 and .wav read instantly. A video (.mov, .mp4) is read by playing it through once, so keep the page open while it runs.
